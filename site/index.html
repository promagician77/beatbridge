Lights on the one: the DJM-A9's clock into Onyx, measured

The DJM-A9 sends a MIDI timing clock over USB: tempo only, no downbeat. Onyx follows that clock in chases and DyLOS, but its FX engine runs from a speed value on a 0–255 fader. That is where moving-head sweeps and pixel FX drift off the beat. BeatBridge reads the clock, holds the bar, and keeps Onyx FX on the "1". Below it runs live against a simulated DJM-A9, or against your own mixer through Web MIDI.

Signal chain and the Onyx side

Onyx build

  1. Beat Provider (Onyx 4.10+): Library › Input Source › Beat Provider, source Midi Clock = DJM-A9. Set Quantum 4 and Beat Control Track.
  2. Bar-locked moving-head looks as Chase cuelists, with Beat/Sound Trigger set to that Beat Provider and Fade % near 100 for continuous movement. Chases take their BPM from the provider natively.
  3. VPL pixels through DyLOS with the Beat Provider in the Sync tab. Patch the VPLs in an 8-bit pixel mode: 48 channels per VPL 305 (16 pixels × RGB), 16-bit only where slow fades need it.
  4. FX-engine looks (circles, fans, pixel waves) each get an Override cue with FX speed stored at max, on its own playback fader. BeatBridge drives that fader over OSC (/Mx/fader/…, 0–255).
  5. Re-phase with a release/go pair on the FX cue at the downbeat, sent ahead by the measured latency. Check on the console that the restart is seamless for each FX.
  6. Calibrate each FX-speed fader once: BeatBridge steps it and times the FX from Onyx's Art-Net output.
  7. Downbeat source: the DJ presses MIDI START on the "1", a DJM button sends a note, or the operator taps.

What the facts say

  • The DJM-A9 sends MIDI timing clock (BPM) even with MIDI ON/OFF off, plus START/STOP from its button. AUTO BPM measures 70–180 BPM and blinks when it can't measure. DJM-A9 manual pp. 48–58
  • The Onyx Beat Provider takes Midi Clock, Ableton Link, Tap Sync or audio input, and feeds Chase cuelists and DyLOS. The manual page doesn't mention the FX engine. Onyx manual: Beat Provider
  • Onyx OSC fader values are integers 0–255 (/Mx/fader/<id>), and playback actions are go, pause, release and select. Onyx forum: OSC documentation
  • Community FX-speed solutions use an Override cue on a fader driven by OSC. Onyx forum threads
  • The SGM VPL 305 has 16 pixels, DMX modes of 3/6/48/96 channels, and Art-Net/sACN through SGM's processor. SGM / dealer spec
  • Ableton Link input since Onyx 4.10 can take tempo and phase from Pioneer's PRO DJ LINK Bridge when CDJs are on the network. Onyx manual, forum
